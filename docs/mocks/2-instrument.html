How long does a repeat prescription usually take to be ready?

Most UK pharmacies have a repeat ready in two to three working days once the surgery has signed it off; same-day is common if the item is in stock and you ask at the counter. If you ordered through the NHS App you’ll see the status change to “ready to collect”.
